About the role

For HR professionals who have operated HRMS tools and managed statutory compliance, and are now ready to step into HR-Tech — configuring, implementing, and enabling these systems for client organisations. You'll work alongside a product and implementation team to map client HR processes into system workflows, configure payroll and compliance modules, and train HR teams.

Key responsibilities

  • End-to-end HRMS implementation (Keka / Darwinbox / GreytHR / Zoho People) — employee master, org structure, approval workflows
  • Configure payroll components, leave, attendance, shift and geo-fencing rules
  • Statutory module set-up: PF, ESI, PT, LWF, Gratuity, TDS slabs, Form 16, Form 24Q, ECR validation
  • Wage Code-compliant salary structure configuration (50% basic norm, etc.)
  • Client onboarding, data migration, gap analysis, SIT/UAT test cases
  • Training client HR teams; preparing user manuals, SOPs, training decks; go-live & hypercare support
  • Maintain BRDs, CRs, defect logs and contribute to internal knowledge base

Must-have skills

  • 6 months – 1 year of hands-on HR Ops, payroll, or compliance experience
  • Practical use of at least one HRMS (Keka / Darwinbox / GreytHR / Zoho People / SAP / ADP)
  • Working knowledge of PF, ESI, PT, LWF, TDS on salary
  • Awareness of Code on Wages 2019 and its impact on payroll structures
  • MS Excel proficiency (VLOOKUP, pivot tables) and strong communication

Good to have

  • Exposure to HRMS implementation, go-live or UAT testing
  • Basic understanding of SaaS SDLC; BRD documentation experience
  • Familiarity with Jira, Zoho Projects, or Trello

Associate – HR-Tech Implementation → HR-Tech Consultant (12–24 months) → Senior Consultant / Module Lead (36+ months)
